See the work, not just the result.

Run a science fair, an olympiad, a hackathon or a club challenge. Participants show their progress as they go, you check it in a few taps, and judges and visitors score from their phones.

One platform, four ways to compete

Science and project fairs
Weeks of building with a progress post each week, then a judging day with stands, judges and visitor votes.
Olympiads and quizzes
Choice, short answer, matching and written questions. Most are marked automatically; the best go through to the next round.
Hackathons and team challenges
Teams sign up together, share one page of progress and hand in their code, drawings or video at the end.
Club and creative challenges
Open calls for a photo, a model or a design. Anyone with the link can enter, and the public picks a favourite.

For participants

Everything about your entry on one page, from the first sketch to the results.

  1. Join in a minute

    Open the invite link, sign in with an account you already have and fill in the entry form.

  2. Post as you build

    A photo from your phone or a few lines of text. You get a reminder before each deadline.

  3. Get feedback early

    See which posts were accepted and read the organiser's comments while there is still time to fix things.

  4. See how you did

    Scores, judges' notes and your whole build history stay in your account after the competition ends.

For organisers

Entries arrive in one list you can sort, check and download. No copying names out of form replies.

A missed week shows up the day after the deadline, not the night before the final, so you can nudge people in time.

If someone posts the same photo twice, it is marked for you to look at. The rest you approve in a couple of taps.

You choose the stages, the dates, who can join and how the winner is decided.

Judging without the clipboard

Judges

Each judge scores their entries on a phone, against the criteria you set. Nothing to collect and add up afterwards.

Visitors

People at the event scan a code and vote in seconds, without making an account.

Results

Scores and votes are combined the way you chose, so you can announce winners before everyone heads home.

Who makes this

Two school students who build things and kept running into sign-up spreadsheets. It is part of Cosmos1562, our space project. Questions and ideas are welcome.
